# Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft — for earliest-stage prototype. Not a substitute for legal advice.

## Data We Collect

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|------|---------|-----------|
| Restaurant/venue profiles | Account and access control | Per venue; deletable on request |
| Bill data (table, items, prices) | Split-by-item checkout | Per transaction; deletable on request |
| Diner selections | Per-item split calculation | Per transaction; not persisted beyond settlement |
| Payment data | Settlement (not yet integrated) | To be defined when payment integration is added |

## Data Storage

- Bill and selection data is stored in the application's database (prototype: local development only).
- No payment data is collected yet (payment integration not built).
- When payment integration is added, PCI DSS compliance will be required.

## Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent (where processing is consent-based)

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the maintainer.
